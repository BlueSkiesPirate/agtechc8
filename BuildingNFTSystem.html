Project Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this project was to utilize hydroponics as a means to grow crops vertically in a semi-controlled enviroment, thereby allowing me to grow crops for a more extensive period of time, utilizing both less space and allowing for more automation.

In a broader spectrum, the issue that my system aims to solve is allowing people who aren't as knowledable in growing crops to start growing their own food with a system that allows for monitoring in ways that aren't traditional to soil growing, like measuring PH, and EC, but with a striaght forward approach. Additionally, my system aims to solve upon an issue known quite commonly among New York residents which is the ever changing weather.

Why choose NFT

Why choose NFT? The reason I chose the NFT is because of it's ease to integrate as a wall build, with other systems like kratsky, deep water culture, aeroponics or even ebb and flow, require the plants to be directly on the water reservoir, or are simply more efficient if done horizontally.

This system was designed to feed 1 person in it's current form but could be expanded to feed more.

A major considertaiong that must be made before starting to build this system or any NFT system at all is the risk of system failure. To elaborate, NFT systems require water pumps, and air, pumps, and a great amount of interconnected parts, this means a high possibility of leaks which could lower the efficiency of the farm, and motor failures which could destroy entire harvests, however, there are ways to mitigate the chnaces of this happening and to even safe the harvest if soemthing does happen and get back to buisness.

The main advantages of NFT, are it's water efficiency, since a system without leaks can keep recycling most of it's water, and the availability of oxygen to the plant roots allowing for less chances of root rot ( a very common plant desease caused by lack of oxygen to the roots).

Due to the water efficiency of this system and it's ability to keep producing crops for a longer period of time out of the year it is a very sustainable choice so long as the neccessary maintence is offered.
